A travel vlog of the classic kind: a group of friends, a run of islands, and
a camera along for the diving, the snorkelling and the days between. Shooting
was the easy part. Back home the trip was 800 GB of clips that had all felt
important on the day, and turning them into a two-hour film in DaVinci
Resolve took several months of evenings: cut for the story of the trip rather
than the itinerary, and graded so the islands look the way they felt.

The film is in Finnish. There are not many photographs from the trip, the
camera was rolling; the stills below are from the film.
