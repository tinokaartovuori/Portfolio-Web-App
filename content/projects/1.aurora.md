The brief was a label identity that could survive being applied a hundred times
a year without a designer touching it. Every release gets a cover generated from
the first thirty seconds of the master — amplitude drives the banding, spectral
centroid drives the hue.

The renderer runs in a worker and writes to an `OffscreenCanvas`, so a release
page can generate its own artwork on load without dropping a frame. The same
code runs at print resolution in a build step for the vinyl sleeves.
