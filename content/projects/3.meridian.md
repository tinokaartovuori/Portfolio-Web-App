The original form asked for everything at once and lost most people at the
payment step. The rebuild asks one question per screen and never blocks on
validation the server has not run yet.

Most of the work was in the transitions. A step change animates the answer into
place rather than replacing the screen, so the flow reads as one continuous
form instead of five separate ones.
