A conductor cannot read a interface mid-phrase, so the whole thing is legible in
peripheral vision: three states, each a different shape rather than a different
colour, at a size that reads from the back of a rehearsal room.

It runs offline as a PWA, because rehearsal spaces are almost always basements.
